pa-fhir-poc

A reference pipeline for the bottleneck of prior-authorization interoperability: getting coverage policy out of PDFs. A local LLM extracts requirements from a Medicare coverage policy, a Neo4j graph holds them as reviewable facts, and a durable Temporal workflow blocks until a named human approves — only then are Da Vinci CRD/DTR FHIR artifacts projected, validated with zero errors against the official HL7 validator.

Why now: the CMS Interoperability and Prior Authorization Final Rule (CMS-0057-F) requires impacted payers to stand up prior-authorization APIs by January 1, 2027 — and every one of them faces the same unsolved first step this pipeline demonstrates: digitizing prose policy into FHIR artifacts, with governance.
